# CattleGate

Herd records software for small and mid-size cattle operations. It runs on your own Windows PC, keeps your records on your own PC, and costs nothing.

**[Download CattleGate for Windows](https://github.com/CattleGate/CattleGate/releases/latest/download/CattleGate-Setup.exe)**

Older versions and release notes are on the [Releases](https://github.com/CattleGate/CattleGate/releases) page.

## Three promises

- **Your data is yours.** Your herd lives in a database on your computer, not on someone else's server. No account, no subscription. Export your herd list and reports to Excel any time.
- **No internet. Ever.** CattleGate never connects to the internet for anything. It doesn't check in, sync or send data anywhere.
- **Free.** No purchase price, no monthly fee and no trial that runs out. Built by a rancher for his own Dexter herd and his daughter's cattle operation, and offered to other ranchers who need good records without enterprise software prices.

## What it does

CattleGate covers the ranch year from calving to the sale barn. Use the parts that fit your operation and skip the rest.

- **The herd:** every animal's tags, registration, calves, weights, treatments, moves and genetics in one record.
- **Breeding:** batch bull turn-in and pull-out, expected calving ranges, preg checks, and herd reproductive status at a glance. Handles year-round bulls and composite sires.
- **Pastures and grazing:** a pasture move log and rotational grazing records with NRCS Practice 528 in mind.
- **Weights:** single and group weigh-ins, average daily gain, and batch weaning.
- **Health:** your own medication catalog, batch treatments, a printable chute checklist, and withdrawal dates tracked.
- **Sales:** sale barn, private treaty and freezer beef sales, with printed receipts.
- **Reports:** print preview on everything and export to Excel.
- **Forgiving:** a Practice Herd to learn on, Reverse on the big batch screens, automatic backups, and spreadsheet import checked row by row.

## Installing

You need a Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit). Everything CattleGate needs is in the one setup file; there's nothing else to download. It doesn't run on a Mac, Chromebook, phone or tablet.

1. Download the setup file with the link above.
2. Run it. Your browser or Windows may warn about a program that few people have downloaded yet. That's expected: choose **Keep** or **Download anyway** in the browser, and **More info > Run anyway** on the blue Windows box.
3. Follow the setup screens. If you've used CattleGate before, say **Yes** when it offers to save a copy of your databases first.

The **CattleGate Installation Guide** (PDF, on the Releases page) walks through every screen. A full User Guide is installed with the program.

**Updating:** download the new setup file and run it. It replaces the program and leaves your databases, backups and settings alone.

## Where your records live

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Databases | `C:\Users\Public\Documents\CattleGate\Databases` |
| Backups | Your Documents folder, `CattleGate\Backups` |
| The program | `C:\Program Files\CattleGate` |

Uninstalling removes only the program. Your records stay put.
